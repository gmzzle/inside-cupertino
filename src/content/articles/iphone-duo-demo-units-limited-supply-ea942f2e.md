When the iPhone Duo goes on sale on October 23, you'll have no trouble finding one to fold open in an Apple Store. Finding one to take home may be harder.

That's the split picture from Bloomberg's Mark Gurman in this week's Power On newsletter. Apple is planning a major in-store push for its first foldable, with an unusually large number of demo units, while Gurman says supply will be limited.

## The demo numbers

Gurman [posted on X](https://x.com/markgurman/status/2109284946900820297) this morning that Apple's Fifth Avenue store in New York is expected to have more than 150 iPhone Duo demo units for launch, and the Apple Park Visitor Center nearly 100. According to [9to5Mac's summary](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/11/apple-planning-huge-iphone-duo-demo-experiences-at-select-retail-stores-report/) of the newsletter, three more big stores, Michigan Avenue in Chicago, The Grove in Los Angeles and the Upper West Side in Manhattan, will each get roughly 30 to 40. A typical non-flagship store is expected to get about 20.

The hardware around those phones is new too. Per [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/11/iphone-duo-retail-showcase/):

- Fifth Avenue will set aside a section of the store for the Duo.
- Most stores will add an extra spotlight table for it.
- A new fixture will show the phone folding and unfolding, so it's hard to miss when you walk in.
- A new MagSafe stand holds an open Duo next to two regular iPhones for side-by-side comparisons.
- Untethered demo phones along the sides of stores will let people try StandBy and other features without a security cable.

## The supply catch

Here's the part that matters if you actually want one. Gurman says supply will be limited. MacRumors reports that about two months ago, Apple thought production problems might keep sales from starting until later in November. It has since built up enough inventory to launch this month as planned, but stock is still expected to be tight.

So the big displays aren't a sign that shelves will be full. If anything, they're the opposite. Apple wants as many people as possible to hold the hinge, see the 7.6-inch inner screen and compare it with an iPhone 18 Pro, even if many of them end up ordering and waiting.

## Why Apple is going this big

A foldable is a hard product to sell from a webpage. Most buyers have never used one, and at $1,999 for the 256GB model, the Duo costs a lot more than most people have ever paid for a phone. People want to feel the crease and the hinge before they spend that.

It also fits a pattern from the last few days. Gurman reported on Friday that U.S. Apple Stores will [greet Duo buyers by name](/articles/iphone-duo-hello-name-greeting-apple-store-5208f3b3/) the first time they turn the phone on, using store equipment that can update sealed boxes. Put that together with a dedicated section at Fifth Avenue and a fixture built just to show the fold, and Apple is clearly steering Duo buyers toward its own stores.

## What to do if you want one

Preorders open Thursday, October 16, at 5 a.m. Pacific. If you already know you want a Duo, that's your best shot at getting one on or near October 23. If you're on the fence, the store visit is a reasonable plan: Apple is going out of its way to let you try one first. Just don't expect to walk out with one the same day, at least not in the first few weeks.
