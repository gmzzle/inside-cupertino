Siri isn't the only assistant on your CarPlay screen anymore. Google [announced on Wednesday](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/) that the Gemini app now runs directly on Apple CarPlay, so iPhone owners can hold a back-and-forth voice conversation with Gemini Live through the car's display and speakers.

Gemini was already reachable in the car through Google Maps, as [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1031981/googles-gemini-app-is-now-available-in-apple-carplay) points out. What's new is a dedicated Gemini icon on the main CarPlay screen, the same treatment Google already gives Android Auto and cars with Google built-in.

## What it does

Google pitches it as a "conversational driving companion." You tap the Gemini icon on the CarPlay screen or side rail, then either pick up a recent chat or start a new one. You can interrupt it to add a detail, change the subject, or pause when you need to focus on an exit.

Google's own examples fall into four buckets:

- **Your day:** "What does my day look like?" or help prepping for a meeting.
- **Your inbox:** a spoken daily brief, unread email catch-ups, summaries of long threads, and talking through a quick reply. This leans on Gemini's connection to Gmail and Google Calendar.
- **Where you're headed:** a quick lunch spot near your destination, or the backstory on a landmark.
- **Thinking out loud:** rehearsing a presentation or breaking down a topic you're curious about.

Every conversation syncs to the Gemini app on your iPhone, so whatever you talked through is in your chat history once you park.

## How to set it up

Google lists three steps on the phone before Gemini shows up in the car:

1. Update the Gemini app on your iPhone to the latest version.
2. Turn on microphone access for the Gemini app.
3. Use Gemini Live on your iPhone at least once.

Then connect to CarPlay and tap the Gemini icon. Step three is the one people will skip. If the icon doesn't appear, open Gemini Live on the phone once and reconnect. Google says this is rolling out globally starting this week, so it may also just take a little while to reach you.

## The fine print

- **Languages:** it's available in "every supported Gemini Live language," according to Google.
- **Daily Brief:** you have to turn it on first. Google says Daily Brief and some other personalization features aren't available to users under 18, or in the European Economic Area and Nigeria.
- **What Google didn't say:** the post doesn't list a minimum iOS version, whether any feature needs a paid Gemini plan, or a way to start a conversation by voice instead of tapping the icon. Test it in the driveway before you count on it for a road trip.

## Our take

This is the clearest sign yet that Apple is letting other assistants into spaces Siri used to own. CarPlay already has a category for voice chatbot apps, and as we noted this week, code in the iOS 27.3 beta suggests the same category is headed to [HomeView](/articles/homeview-security-camera-ai-chatbot-apps-4b8e2d17/).

For people who live in Gmail and Google Calendar, Gemini on CarPlay is easy to justify: it already plugs into the inbox and calendar you actually use. The catch is that tapping an icon to start is still one more glance at the screen than a wake word. Keep your requests short, and let it wait at red lights when the conversation gets long.
