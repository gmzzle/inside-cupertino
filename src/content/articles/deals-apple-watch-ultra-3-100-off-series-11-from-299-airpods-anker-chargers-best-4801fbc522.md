Amazon just knocked $100 off the Apple Watch Ultra 3, which is interesting timing considering we're barely into August. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/03/deals-apple-watch-ultra-3-100-off-airpods-anker-chargers/) spotted the discount alongside refreshed Series 11 pricing starting at $299, and the whole thing feels less like a summer sale and more like inventory management.

The Ultra 3 launched in September 2025 at $799, and discounts this deep usually don't show up until retailers need to clear shelf space for new models. We're exactly one month from Apple's typical September event window. Either Amazon's making a bet on what gets announced, or the Ultra 3 simply isn't moving units the way Apple expected. The Series 11 discount makes more sense—that's the mass-market model, and $299 puts it in impulse-buy range for back-to-school shoppers.

What's more telling is that AirPods Pro 3 are still $50 off and the AirTag 2 is at all-time low pricing. Apple's summer product refresh was minimal this year, which usually means fall is going to be busy. When retailers start discounting current-gen gear this aggressively, they're clearing the deck.

The Ultra line has always been Apple's toughest sell. It's essentially a Series with a bigger battery and a titanium case, marketed to people who hike twice a year. The action button was supposed to be the killer feature, but most owners just use it to start workouts—something a $299 Series 11 does fine. Apple keeps positioning the Ultra as essential gear for serious athletes, but serious athletes already have Garmin watches that blow it away on battery life and don't require an iPhone.

If you've been waiting on an Ultra 3, $100 off is probably as good as it gets before the next model arrives and this one becomes last year's titanium paperweight.
