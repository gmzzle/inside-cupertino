[9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/09/homekit-weekly-onvis-releases-a-matter-over-thread-outdoor-smart-plug-with-energy-monitoring/) spotted the new Onvis outdoor smart plug, and it's genuinely the first one I've seen that doesn't force you to pick your poison. Matter over Thread? Check. Energy monitoring? Check. Weather-resistant enough to leave outside year-round? IP65, so yes. No weird proprietary hub sitting on your shelf collecting dust? Also yes.

This matters because outdoor smart plugs have been a compromise factory. You either get Wi-Fi (slow, unreliable, eats battery on your phone when you're configuring it) or you get Zigbee with a hub you have to manage separately. Thread fixes the mesh networking problem without the hub tax, and Matter means it actually talks to Apple Home without requiring a firmware update every time Apple sneezes. The energy monitoring is table stakes at this point—if you're plugging in holiday lights or a pool pump, knowing what it's costing you is the difference between convenience and a surprise electric bill.

Timing's smart too. October launch means you can actually test this thing before you're standing on a ladder in 40-degree weather trying to troubleshoot why your Christmas lights won't turn on via Siri. The IP65 rating means it'll handle rain and snow, though you still shouldn't submerge it or point a pressure washer at it. Thread's local communication also means it'll keep working even if your internet goes down, which is more useful than it sounds when you've got outdoor security lighting on a schedule.

The real test will be whether Thread performs as advertised in outdoor installations. Thread's range is theoretically better than Bluetooth but worse than Wi-Fi, and most people's Thread networks are still pretty thin—a couple HomePod minis and maybe an Apple TV if they're lucky. If your outdoor plug is 50 feet from your nearest Thread border router, through walls and weather, you might still have problems. But at least you won't be troubleshooting a separate hub when it inevitably stops responding at 11 PM on a Saturday.
