The iPhone Duo launched yesterday, and [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/23/iphone-duo-accessories-here-are-five-top-picks-for-apples-foldable/) is already rounding up accessory picks. That's faster than usual, which tells you Apple seeded a lot of third-party manufacturers early. They learned that lesson from the original iPhone, when cases didn't exist for months and people were wrapping $600 phones in Saran wrap.

The accessory challenge here is obvious: the Duo folds. That hinge means traditional cases don't work, screen protectors become a nightmare, and MagSafe has to somehow stay attached through the fold without adding bulk. Apple's own first-party case is $129, which is steep until you remember it has to protect a $2,499 device with a screen that costs $799 to replace. The math works, but it still stings.

What's interesting is how third parties are solving the hinge problem differently. Some are going with a two-piece design that leaves the hinge exposed—lighter, but you're one drop away from explaining to AppleCare what happened. Others are doing a full wrap that adds millimeters but actually protects the thing. There's no clear winner yet because nobody's dropped enough of these to know what fails first. Samsung's foldable ecosystem took two years to figure this out, and Apple's coming in late enough to skip some mistakes but early enough that we're still in the "let's see what breaks" phase.

The screen protector situation is worse. The Duo's ultra-thin glass can't handle the adhesives that normal protectors use, so you're either going with Apple's $49 film that needs professional installation or risking it naked. There's no good third option yet, and Apple knows it—they're already offering free protector replacements for the first year if you buy AppleCare+. That's not generosity, that's acknowledgment.

The real question is whether any of this matters to the people who bought a Duo on day one. If you're spending $2,500 on a folding phone, you're probably spending another $200 on the full accessory kit without blinking.
