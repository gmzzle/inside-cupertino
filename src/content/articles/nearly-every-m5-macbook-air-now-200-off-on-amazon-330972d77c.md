[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/01/m5-macbook-air-200-off/) spotted $200 discounts across nearly the entire M5 MacBook Air lineup on Amazon this weekend. The 13-inch starts at $1,099.99 for 512GB, and the 15-inch at $1,299.99. All colors, all storage configs.

This matters because Apple raised base prices when the M5 Air launched in March. The old M4 Air started at $999 for 256GB, but Apple killed that tier entirely and made 512GB the new entry point at $1,299. It was a quiet $100 increase disguised as a storage bump—classic Apple margin engineering. So a $200 discount doesn't just beat launch pricing; it actually undercuts what you would've paid for last year's model with equivalent storage.

The timing's also telling. We're eight months into the M5 Air's cycle, and Apple just launched the M5 Pro MacBook Pro last month. That means the Air is officially mid-cycle hardware, and retailers know it. These aren't desperate clearance prices—the M5 Air is still excellent and will be current through next spring—but they're the first acknowledgment that the launch premium is over. If you've been waiting, this is the window.

One thing worth noting: the base 16GB RAM config is plenty for most people, but the 24GB option is also $200 off at $1,599. That's the same discount, which makes it a better deal if you're even slightly RAM-curious. Apple charges $200 to go from 16GB to 24GB, so you're effectively getting that upgrade at cost when it's on sale. Not a bad hedge if you plan to keep this thing for five years.

Bottom line: if you need an Air and weren't thrilled about paying $1,299 for the base model, now's the time. This is the first discount that actually moves the needle.
