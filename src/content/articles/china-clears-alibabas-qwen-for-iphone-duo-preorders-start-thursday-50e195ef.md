China's telecom filings just put Alibaba's AI models on the same permit paperwork as Apple Intelligence for the China-model iPhone Duo — days before local preorders open.

[Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4235207/china-clears-alibabas-qwen-ai-just-in-time-for-iphone-duo.html), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3370893/china-approves-alibaba-ai-models-use-iphone-duo-and-other-apple-handsets), and [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34250010) report that China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) network-access records for the China iPhone Duo (model A3721) now list three large-model registration numbers on a permit refreshed September 29. The filings were widely covered on October 14.

## What the Duo permit lists

The three registrations reported on the Duo filing are:

1. **Shanghai-AppleZhiNeng-202506160057** — Apple Intelligence
2. **Shanghai-TongYiWanXiang-202410090024** — Alibaba Tongyi Wanxiang (image)
3. **ZheJiang-TongYiQianWen-20230901** — Alibaba Tongyi Qianwen (Qwen)

That is the regulatory breadcrumb trail, not an Apple marketing page. Apple Intelligence for China had already appeared in earlier model filings. What is new is Alibaba's Qwen and Wanxiang showing up alongside it on the Duo and, according to the same reports, on other China-bound iPhones including the iPhone 18 Pro Max (A3718), with coverage also flagging the 18 Pro series, 17 Pro, and Air.

## Why Alibaba is in the picture

Apple has been building a China-specific AI stack because mainland rules require large models used on devices to clear Chinese registration. Alibaba has said its Qwen family will integrate into Apple Intelligence for China users across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS, covering text and image understanding and generation. Wanxiang is the image-generation side of that pairing.

None of today's filing coverage invents a public ship date for the software. Apple still has not publicly timed the full China Apple Intelligence rollout. The filings say the models are cleared for those handsets' network-access paperwork. They do not say the features are live in a China software update tomorrow.

## Timing: Duo preorders are this week

The regulatory update lands right against the China Duo calendar. China Duo preorders are reported to open October 16 around 8 p.m. China time, with shipping October 23. Local start pricing has been reported at 15,999 yuan. In the U.S., [Get Ready for Duo preorders](/articles/iphone-duo-get-ready-preorders-oct-16-c4e91a70/) is already open ahead of October 16 at 5:00 a.m. PT.

So China buyers get a foldable launch week that also happens to carry the clearest public signal yet that Apple Intelligence there will lean on Alibaba's registered models — not the same foundation models used in regions where Apple Intelligence is already shipping.

## China AI vs. everywhere else

Outside mainland China, Apple Intelligence rides Apple's own models and partners that cleared different regulatory paths. Inside China, the MIIT large-model registrations are the gate. Listing AppleZhiNeng, Tongyi Qianwen, and Tongyi Wanxiang on the Duo permit is how that gate looks in practice for the foldable.

That split matters for what China users will eventually see. Text and image features may share the Apple Intelligence brand and UI shell, but the underlying models — and therefore behavior, language coverage, and content policies — will follow the China-registered stack. Southern Metropolis and other local outlets covering the filings today frame the same point: the paperwork is catching up to a market where on-device AI has to clear domestic registration before it ships widely.

## Cook in China, filings on paper

Computerworld notes Tim Cook — now executive chairman in that outlet's framing — was in China this week meeting Vice Premier He Lifeng on Monday. Treat that as background, not a causal claim. The MIIT permit refresh is dated September 29; the filings stand on their own as the story for Duo buyers.

## What to watch next

- **China Duo order window:** October 16 preorders, October 23 ship, start price reported at 15,999 yuan.
- **Software timing:** still unset publicly. A telecom-permit clearance is not an iOS China feature drop with a date on Apple's site.
- **Other handsets:** the same model-registration updates are reported beyond Duo, including 18 Pro Max and broader Pro / Air coverage — a China portfolio move, not a foldable-only footnote.

If you are buying Duo in China, the hardware calendar is set. The AI calendar is closer than it was, but Apple still has to flip the software switch. Until then, the filings are the clearest public proof that Qwen, Wanxiang, and Apple Intelligence are cleared for the same China iPhones.
