[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/27/2026-macbook-pro-900-off/) spotted a $900 discount on the 16-inch M5 Max MacBook Pro at Amazon, dropping a $4,399 config to $3,499. That's a massive cut for a machine that only launched in June. For context, Apple almost never sees discounts this steep within the same calendar year, let alone the same quarter.

The backstory matters here. Apple raised MacBook Pro prices across the board this summer—the first meaningful increase since the M1 transition in 2020. The 16-inch M5 Max with 36GB RAM and 2TB storage launched at $4,399, up from $3,899 for the comparable M4 Max config last year. That's a $500 hike justified by, well, Apple didn't really justify it. They just did it. Now Amazon is effectively erasing that increase and then some.

This isn't normal clearance behavior. These aren't last year's models gathering dust in a warehouse. The M5 Max is the current flagship chip, and we're still three months out from any rumored M6 announcement. Either Amazon ordered way too much inventory and is eating the loss to move units, or Apple's pricing experiment failed harder than anyone expected. The fact that multiple M5 Max configs are being discounted simultaneously—the 48GB/2TB model is $500 off—suggests the latter.

It's also worth noting what's *not* on sale: the base M5 Pro models are holding steady. The discounts are concentrated on the Max configurations, which start at $3,499 even before this sale. Apple clearly overestimated how many people would pay $4,400 for a laptop when the M5 Pro at $2,899 handles 90% of pro workflows just fine. The Max has always been a niche product, but Apple priced it into an even smaller niche this year.

If you've been waiting on an M5 Max, this is your window. But the bigger story is that Apple might need to rethink what the market will actually bear for these machines.
