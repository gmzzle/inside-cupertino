[AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/25/mac-studio-gets-update-to-m5-max-and-m5-ultra?utm_source=rss) reported the Mac Studio update today, and the specs are what you'd expect: M5 Max and M5 Ultra options, the usual performance multipliers in Apple's press materials, and Johny Srouji calling it "our most powerful Mac ever" as if that phrase hasn't appeared in every pro Mac launch since 2020.

The interesting bit is the 16-month gap since the M4 update in April 2025. That's not quite annual, not quite the 18-month pro cycle Apple sometimes falls into. It suggests the M5 generation came together faster than usual, probably because the chip architecture from M4 to M5 was more iterative than the M3-to-M4 jump. The Studio has always been the awkward middle child in Apple's desktop lineup - too expensive for most people who'd be fine with a Mac mini, not modular enough for the Mac Pro crowd who actually need PCIe slots. Sixteen months between updates keeps it relevant without requiring Apple to reinvent the chassis every year.

The performance claims are hard to evaluate without independent testing, but "4.3 times faster AI performance" likely means they're comparing the new Neural Accelerators in the M5 GPU against the M4's neural engine in a specific workload that highlights the new architecture. Real-world gains will be smaller. The 2x storage speed is more straightforward - probably a controller upgrade - and actually matters if you're working with large video files or running local AI models that hit the SSD constantly.

What's missing from this launch is any hint about the Mac Pro. The M5 Ultra in the Studio uses the same chip the Mac Pro would presumably get, and if Apple's releasing it now in the Studio, the Pro is either coming very soon or Apple's decided the $6,999 expandable tower is basically done. The Studio at $1,999 base with an Ultra option makes the Mac Pro's existence harder to justify every year. This update doesn't clarify that - it just makes the question louder.
