Launch day is here for Apple's two new home devices. The all-new [HomeView](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/meet-apple-homeview-a-new-way-to-enjoy-entertainment-and-manage-the-home/) smart display and the fourth-generation Apple TV 4K both go on sale today, Friday, November 13, after a month of pre-orders. Each costs $299 in the U.S.

If one is landing on your doorstep today, or you're thinking about walking into a store for one, here's what's worth knowing first.

## Getting one today

Ordering online won't get you either device soon. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-4k-and-homeview-now-available-heres-everything-you-need-to-know/) reports that Apple's online store shows both HomeView (in all four colors) and the new Apple TV 4K backordered to the first week of December. Most Apple Stores appear to have same-day pickup, especially for HomeView in silver and starlight, so a store visit is the faster route this weekend.

HomeView is on sale in the U.S., Canada, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, India, Singapore and South Africa. It is not sold in the European Union, because Siri AI isn't available on iOS there. Apple says it is "working constructively with the EU in an attempt to find a path forward" but hasn't given a timeline.

## HomeView: update before you do anything else

HomeView ships with HomeView Software 26.5, which [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/12/apple-homeview-software-27-2-24v526/) notes is about six months old, and setup will ask you to install an update. Let it run. Apple released HomeView Software 27.2 (build 24V526) on Thursday, and its [security notes](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-homeview-launches-tomorrow-but-its-first-security-update-is-already-here/) list fixes for publicly disclosed bugs, including one that could let a remote attacker join an iMessage group chat and one that could let a malicious Bluetooth device pair without any user interaction.

## HomeView: voice unlock isn't ready yet

If you ask Siri on HomeView to unlock the front door or open the garage and it refuses, your lock isn't broken. Apple [told reviewer Joanna Stern](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/12/apple-plans-homeview-update-with-siri-fix/) that a software update next month will let Siri AI control security accessories for people HomeView has recognized. Until then, open the Home app on the HomeView screen and use the on-screen control. Apple hasn't given an exact December date.

We covered how HomeView's face and voice recognition keeps camera and microphone data on the device [yesterday](/articles/homeview-secure-exclaves-person-recognition-c84e92f3/).

## HomeView: the accessories are trickling in

The first three accessories in Apple's HomeView-compatible Apple Home Collection can be [ordered starting today](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/13/apple-home-collection-orders-begin/): the LG Indoor Cam ($199.95), LG Outdoor Cam ($299.95) and LG Video Doorbell ($249.95). The rest of the LG and Schneider Home lineup is expected by early 2027.

If you bought the wall-mounted HomeView, remember the mount is a separate purchase. Schneider Home's Power Mount USB-C Wall Connector starts at $59.95 and the Power Mount AC Wired at $79.95. The Power Mount PoE Wired doesn't arrive until early 2027.

## Apple TV 4K: what $299 gets you

The new Apple TV 4K moves to the A19 chip, up from the A15 Bionic in the last model, and comes with 128GB of storage and Wi-Fi 7. It adds far-field microphones, so you can talk to Siri without picking up the remote, and a new Siri Remote with Find My.

Its other headline feature is surround sound from four HomePods. You need four HomePod (2nd generation) or HomePod mini 2 speakers, set up as matching pairs at the front and back of the room.

## Our take

For the Apple TV, the important detail is the one that argues against buying it. 9to5Mac notes that Siri AI is also coming to the second- and third-generation Apple TV 4K through a software update. If your current box works and you don't plan to buy four HomePods, $299 is a lot to pay for a faster chip and hands-free Siri.

HomeView is the more interesting launch, and the safer play is to buy it for what works today. Facial recognition, the Home app and FaceTime work on day one, and voice unlocking comes later. Install the update during setup, keep your normal way of unlocking the door, and judge the Siri fixes when Apple publishes the December release notes.
