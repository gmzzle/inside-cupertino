If you've been holding money aside for Apple hardware this fall, the next four Fridays are about to get busy.

In this weekend's *Power On*, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says Apple is planning a "reset" at its retail stores from the evening of **Thursday, November 5** into **Friday, November 6**, which implies new signage and new products going onto store tables. Since Apple's next round of Mac announcements is expected the week before, he reads November 6 as the day at least some of those machines go on sale. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/18/new-macbook-pro-with-touchscreen-and-oled-november-6-release/), [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/10/18/retail-reset-set-for-november-6-as-oled-macbook-pro-looms) and [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/18/apple-product-releases-next-four-fridays/) all picked it up today.

Slot that in with the dates Apple has already announced and you get four launch Fridays back to back.

## The calendar

- **Friday, October 23: iPhone Duo.** Official. Pre-orders opened October 16 and [slipped to January 25](/articles/iphone-duo-backordered-january-25-3e9d1b60/) within half an hour, so anyone ordering now is waiting until 2027.
- **Friday, October 30: HomePod mini 2.** Official, $149. MacRumors says it's still widely available for launch-day delivery in the U.S. Siri AI won't be on it at launch; Apple says that comes in a later software update.
- **Friday, November 6: new Macs, and maybe an iPad.** Not announced. This is the date Gurman's store-reset detail points to.
- **Friday, November 13: [HomeView and the new Apple TV 4K](/articles/homeview-apple-tv-299-november-13-homepod-mini-2-304c39ff/).** Official, $299 each. Some tabletop HomeView colors and the Apple TV have already slipped to late November or early December delivery in parts of the U.S., per MacRumors.

## What's expected on November 6

None of this is confirmed by Apple yet. Based on current reporting, the candidates are:

- **The redesigned MacBook Pro** with a Tandem OLED touchscreen, reportedly a small Dynamic Island, and a body Gurman has called [significantly lighter](/articles/macbook-pro-redesign-significantly-lighter-bf2c87eb/). 9to5Mac's summary of the reporting says it sticks with the **M5 Pro and M5 Max**, because Apple is skipping the high-end M6 chips to speed up M7.
- **An M6 update for the 14-inch MacBook Pro and the iMac**, according to MacRumors' summary of Gurman.
- **An iPad mini 8 with an OLED display**, which AppleInsider says should also move the front camera to the landscape edge.

MacRumors hedges the list with an "and/or," and so should you. Gurman's case for November 6 rests on the store reset and Apple's habit of shipping on the Friday after an announcement, not on any word from Apple.

## When we'll actually know

The announcements are expected on **Tuesday, October 27 or Wednesday, October 28**. Gurman says Apple has begun inviting media to an October 28 "launch experience" in Los Angeles. That's when prices, chips and real ship dates should firm up.

## What this means if you're buying

- **Thinking about a Mac?** Wait ten days. Buying a MacBook Pro or iMac this week, with replacements likely announced on October 27 or 28, is the easiest way to regret a purchase.
- **Want the OLED MacBook Pro but worried about the chip?** That's the real question. If the reporting holds, the first touchscreen model launches on M5-generation silicon, and a lot of people will want to see how that compares with whatever Apple says about M7 timing.
- **Stacking home gear?** HomePod mini 2 is the one you can still get on day one. If HomeView is on your list, order sooner rather than later, since its delivery windows are already moving.

Four Fridays in a row would be an unusually dense stretch for Apple, and it puts a lot of hardware into the holiday window at once. The October 27–28 announcements will tell us whether the November 6 piece of it is real.
