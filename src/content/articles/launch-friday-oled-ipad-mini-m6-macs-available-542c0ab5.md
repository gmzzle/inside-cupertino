The [four launch Fridays](/articles/apple-four-launch-fridays-november-6-macs-5c81d2a4/) stretch just hit its Mac-and-mini payday. As of Friday, November 6, Apple's [OLED iPad mini](/articles/new-ipad-mini-oled-a20-pro-ip67-7f579175/), [M6 iMac and 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro](/articles/m6-macbook-pro-imac-preorders-45bca586/), and [touchscreen PROLED MacBook Pros](/articles/touchscreen-macbook-pro-oled-35efd89c/) are available to buy from Apple's online store and the Apple Store app.

[MacRumors reports](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/06/ipad-mini-oled-and-m6-imac-available-today/) that the first pre-order deliveries are arriving today, and units are reaching Apple Store locations and Apple Authorized Resellers. [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/11/06/apples-first-deliveries-offer-a-closer-look-at-new-ipad-mini-macbook-pros-and-imac) adds that U.S. customers should start seeing Friday-morning deliveries as carriers run their routes, with in-store pickup and walk-in purchases opening the same day.

This is the availability story, not another announcement deep-dive. Here's what you can buy today, what it costs, and where the wait still is.

## OLED iPad mini (8th gen)

Apple's smallest tablet finally gets OLED. Per MacRumors' launch-day rundown:

- **8.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED**, up to 1,200 nits peak, with a nano-texture option on 256GB and 512GB models
- **A20 Pro** and **N1** (Wi-Fi 7 / Bluetooth 6)
- **5.4 mm / 255 g**, with **IP67** sealing and Acoustic Display audio (no speaker holes)
- Landscape **12MP Center Stage** camera; Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C)

**U.S. pricing:** $699 (Wi-Fi 128GB), $799 / $999 for 256GB / 512GB, +$100 for nano-texture on those configs, cellular from $849, education from $649.

The main early-review knock is still refresh rate: **60Hz, no ProMotion**. If smooth scrolling is non-negotiable, try one in a store before you lock in an order.

## M6 iMac

First iMac update since M4. MacRumors says the M6 brings up to a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, with Apple claiming up to 2x faster graphics versus the prior generation, plus Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and optional 2.5Gb Ethernet on the power adapter.

**Pricing:** from $1,499 (10-core CPU / 10-core GPU) and $1,699 (12/12); education from $1,449.

## 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6

The non-touch base Pro. From **$1,999**, with a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, plus N1 Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Same starting price as before the chip bump — the real decision today is whether you want this machine or the pricier touchscreen PROLED models below.

## Touchscreen MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR

The headline hardware of the week: 14-inch from **$2,899**, 16-inch from **$3,399**, with M5 Pro or M5 Max, a Dynamic Island in place of the notch, and up to 2,000 nits for both SDR and HDR.

### The catch: online ship times

Here's the new angle versus the announcement posts. MacRumors says Apple's online checkout currently shows **many Space Black and Silver touchscreen configurations shipping in 2–3 weeks**. If you want one sooner, a walk-in Apple Store is the better bet for base configs that are typically on hand.

That's the opposite of last month's iPhone Duo scramble, where walk-in stock evaporated. For these Macs, the store may be faster than the truck.
