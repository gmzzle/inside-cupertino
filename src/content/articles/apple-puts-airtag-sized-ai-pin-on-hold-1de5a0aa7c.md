[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/22/apple-pauses-ai-pin-development/) reports Apple has stopped work on an AirTag-sized AI pin with a camera, aluminum and glass body, and the ability to answer questions about your surroundings through Siri. The device was supposed to clip to your clothes or hang around your neck. It's now on hold indefinitely.

This is the right call. The Humane AI Pin launched in 2024 to brutal reviews and worse sales, proving that people do not want a second screen-free device when their phone already does everything better. Apple clearly watched that implosion and decided not to repeat it. The form factor makes no sense when you're already carrying an iPhone with better cameras, faster processing, and a screen you can actually see. Adding a dedicated wearable just fragments the experience.

What's interesting is what Apple is still working on. The company has camera-equipped AirPods in development for 2027, smart glasses prototypes, and a fitness band without a screen. Those make more strategic sense. AirPods already live in your ears all day. Glasses sit on your face where cameras actually point at what you're looking at. A fitness band competes with the Apple Watch at a lower price point, not with the iPhone. The AI pin competed with nothing and solved no problem.

Apple's also been testing Visual Intelligence features in iOS 18.2, which lets you point your iPhone camera at something and get contextual information. That's the use case the AI pin was supposedly built for, except it works on hardware 1.4 billion people already own. Why would Apple cannibalize that with a $200 accessory that does less?

Smart move to kill this early, before any supply chain commitments or marketing spend. Sometimes the best product is the one you don't ship.
