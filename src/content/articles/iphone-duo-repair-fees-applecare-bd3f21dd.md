Two days before preorders, Apple finally put a price on breaking an iPhone Duo. If you skip AppleCare+, a cracked inner display costs $999, and Apple's catch-all "other damage" fee is $1,499.

[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/14/iphone-duo-repair-fees/) spotted the new out-of-warranty estimates on Wednesday. AppleCare+ deductibles for the Duo were already public. Today's update fills in the side most buyers hadn't seen: what Apple charges when you pay for the repair yourself.

## Without AppleCare+

These are Apple's estimated U.S. repair fees for the iPhone Duo without coverage:

- **Cover layer damage:** $49
- **Back glass damage:** $199
- **Battery service:** $229
- **Rear camera damage:** $229
- **Outer display damage:** $379
- **Outer display and back glass damage:** $509
- **Inner display damage:** $999
- **Other damage:** $1,499

The Duo starts at $1,999. That makes the inner-display fee half the base price of the phone, and "other damage" 75 percent of it, as [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/10/14/apples-1499-iphone-duo-repair-cost-is-a-strong-argument-for-applecare) points out. AppleInsider notes that "other damage" typically covers damage that can't be fixed with a simple component swap.

The best news on the list is that $49 cover layer. Apple has already said the nano-texture layer over the inner display can be removed and replaced. If a scratch or ding stops at that layer and the panel underneath is fine, you pay $49, not $999.

## With AppleCare+

AppleCare+ for the Duo costs **$19.99 a month, $199.99 a year, or $399 upfront**. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1011245/iphone-duo-repair-costs-applecare) notes those prices are for AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss. With coverage, the fees drop to:

- **Battery service (below 80% capacity):** $0
- **Cover layer damage:** $19
- **Back glass damage:** $29
- **Outer display damage:** $29
- **Inner display damage:** $99
- **Other accidental damage:** $99
- **Theft or loss:** $149

Those deductibles match the iPhone rows on Apple's [AppleCare+ fees and deductibles page](https://www.apple.com/legal/applecare/fees-deductibles/). That page lists separate tiers for cover-layer-only damage ($19) and inner display damage ($99), so the foldable's extra parts get their own deductibles.

## The math on one bad drop

The inner screen is where the numbers stop being close.

- **No coverage:** $999 for one inner-display repair.
- **AppleCare+ upfront:** $399 plus a $99 deductible, or $498.
- **A year of AppleCare+:** $199.99 plus $99, or about $299.

So one inner-screen repair costs more without coverage than the plan and the deductible together. For "other damage," AppleCare+ takes $1,400 off the bill, from $1,499 to $99. If you would rather self-insure, a cracked outer display ($379) or back glass ($199) won't ruin you. The folding panel is the part that will.

## How it compares to Samsung

[Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/iphone-duo-screen-repair-costs-3723480/), citing Samsung's U.S. repair pricing page, says an inner-screen repair on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 costs $419, and $479 on the Fold 8 Ultra. Samsung's bigger screen module replacements, which include connected parts, are $579 and $639. Even the highest of those is well under Apple's $999.

## Before Friday

Preorders open Friday, October 16, at 5:00 a.m. PT, and the Duo ships October 23. AppleCare+ is one of the choices in Apple's [Get Ready flow](/articles/iphone-duo-get-ready-preorders-oct-16-c4e91a70/), so you can settle it now instead of at 5 a.m. With [supply expected to be tight](/articles/iphone-duo-demo-units-limited-supply-ea942f2e/), that's one less thing to decide when the order button goes live.

Your carrier plan or credit card may already cover some of this, so check before you add a second plan. If not, Apple's own numbers make the case: on a $1,999 foldable, the inner screen is the part you want covered.
