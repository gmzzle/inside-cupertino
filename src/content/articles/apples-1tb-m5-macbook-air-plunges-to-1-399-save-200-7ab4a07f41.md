[AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/30/apples-1tb-m5-macbook-air-plunges-to-1399-save-200?utm_source=rss) spotted a $200 discount on the 1TB M5 MacBook Air, bringing it down to $1,399 on Amazon. That's the 13-inch model with 16GB of RAM and the 10-core GPU, in Sky Blue if you care about that sort of thing.

Here's the thing: this is the config most people should be buying in the first place. The base M5 Air still ships with 256GB of storage in 2026, which is borderline insulting for a machine that starts at $1,199. You'll fill that drive with the OS, a couple Adobe apps, and maybe one decent photo library before you're shopping for external SSDs. Meanwhile, the 512GB model runs $1,399 normally, so this deal gets you double the storage for the same price. It's not revolutionary, but it's the difference between a laptop that works and one that nags you about disk space every six months.

The M5 itself is a modest bump over the M4 - better Neural Engine, slightly faster single-core performance, same 16GB base RAM that arrived last year. Nothing here changes the Air's identity as the sensible default for most people. But storage has always been Apple's favorite upsell lever, and they've kept the base tier embarrassingly low while pretending cloud storage fixes everything. It doesn't.

This discount won't last forever, and $1,399 for a 1TB Air is legitimately the sweet spot if you're buying right now. Just know that you're paying what should've been the normal price all along.
