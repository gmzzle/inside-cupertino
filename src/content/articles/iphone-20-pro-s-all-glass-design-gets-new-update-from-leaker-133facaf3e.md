Apple is apparently planning an all-glass design for the iPhone 20 Pro models in 2026, according to [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/iphone-20-pros-all-glass-design-gets-new-update-from-leaker/). The timing makes sense—it's the iPhone's 20th anniversary, so of course they'd do something visually dramatic. But let's be honest: an all-glass phone is a phone that's going to shatter spectacularly.

The current iPhone design already has a glass back, which Apple added in 2017 for wireless charging and has kept around ever since. Going all-glass presumably means ditching the titanium or stainless frame for glass sides too, which would make the thing feel like a slippery bar of soap wrapped in AppleCare. Yes, it would look incredible on a white showroom table. It would also look incredible in a thousand pieces on your kitchen floor.

There's precedent here, sort of. The iPhone 4 had glass on both sides with a metal band around the edge, and people loved how it looked until they realized how easily it cracked. Apple's glass has gotten tougher since then—Ceramic Shield is legitimately better than what came before—but physics is still physics. More glass means more surface area that can break, and replacing glass is expensive even when Apple controls the entire repair chain.

The anniversary angle is doing a lot of work here. Apple loves a good symbolic redesign, and 20 years is a big number. But the iPhone 4's glass-sandwich design was also about making the phone thinner and enabling new antennas, not just aesthetics. If the iPhone 20 Pro is all-glass just to look pretty, that's a harder sell. If it enables better wireless charging, improved camera performance, or some other functional leap, then maybe the fragility trade-off is worth it.

Either way, AppleCare+ is going to have a very good year in 2026.
