A day after [iPhone Duo preorders blew past Christmas](/articles/iphone-duo-backordered-january-25-3e9d1b60/), Tim Cook was in Shanghai playing one.

On the afternoon of October 16, Apple's executive chairman spent about **45 minutes** at miHoYo with the *Zenless Zone Zero* team, according to [Shanghai Securities News](https://emwap.eastmoney.com/a/202610163898708121.html) (also carried by [NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-10-16/4607141.html) and [Yuncaijing](http://www.yuncaijing.com/news/id_17195129.html)). Co-founder Liu Wei met him at the entrance. Producer Zhenyu walked him through the demo.

Cook later posted on Weibo, in English and Chinese: "Game developers are loving iPhone Duo! So great to hear about the creative process behind Zenless Zone Zero and how miHoYo are designing their game for the first foldable iPhone." [Sina](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5354708710722347.html) preserved the post.

## The fold is the point

Shanghai Securities News says the visit was staged as a three-year timeline of *Zenless Zone Zero* on Apple hardware, with the Duo as the finale. Staff started on the **outer display** — exploration and character progression — then unfolded the phone so combat jumped to the **inner screen**.

That is the pitch Apple wants for a $1,999 foldable: not two phones glued together, but one game that changes shape with the chassis. Zhenyu told the paper that adaptation and optimization for Apple devices is ongoing, and that the studio has "always tightly combined with Apple" and embraces new platform features.

## Version 3.3 is the software drop

The same report says *Zenless Zone Zero* will **lead with iPhone Duo adaptations during version 3.3**. That lines up with what Korean coverage of the visit summarized as Duo compatibility arriving in that update, with more optimization details still to come.

Cook did not announce a ship date for 3.3, and miHoYo has not published a public English changelog tied to the visit. Treat the Duo work as confirmed in direction, not as a day-one App Store listing yet.

## Why this visit matters now

Cook has been on a China swing. Earlier stops included Guangzhou and an iPhone 18 Pro camera bit; the miHoYo stop puts the **foldable** in front of one of the App Store's biggest game partners right as U.S. Duo delivery dates sit in late January.

It is also a soft answer to the supply-story hangover. Apple cannot manufacture Duos faster overnight. It *can* show that major studios are already designing around the inner display — the same argument it made years ago when big games first treated the iPhone as more than a casual device.

For players: if you somehow landed a Duo for October 23, watch the 3.3 patch notes. If you are staring at a January ship date, this is one more reason the wait might still be worth it for gaming, not just for the novelty of a hinge.

## What to watch

- **Zenless Zone Zero 3.3 notes.** The first public list of Duo-specific controls, frame-rate targets, or layout changes.
- **Other China partners.** Cook's Weibo framed this as developer enthusiasm, plural. More studio stops would turn a photo-op into a campaign.
- **App Store featuring.** A Duo gaming collection after launch day would tell you Cupertino is treating foldable play as a category, not a one-off demo.
