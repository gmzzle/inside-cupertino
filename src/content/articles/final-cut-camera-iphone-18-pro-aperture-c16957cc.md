This morning was the security fire drill. This afternoon is the delayed software that should have shipped with the new Pros.

**Final Cut Camera** just picked up the feature Apple spent the September event selling: **variable aperture** on the **iPhone 18 Pro** Fusion Main camera. Creators can dial aperture directly, or flip between **aperture priority** and **shutter priority**, instead of treating that hardware as a marketing slide.

## What actually landed in Final Cut Camera

Apple redesigned the app so pro controls are not buried: **shutter angle**, **ISO**, and **white balance** sit closer to the shoot. Exposure tooling gets customizable overlays with **false color** and clipping indicators, plus improved **focus peaking** with sensitivity control. A **tally light** shows when you are rolling. A two-finger tap clears the viewfinder when you need the frame, not the chrome.

There is also support for professional **video scopes**, **clean SDI out**, and **genlock offset** — the kind of multicam / set language that only matters if you are past TikTok-default Camera.app.

On the creative side, the update unlocks **Cinematic mode effects** for regular video on iPhone 18 Pro, including footage captured in formats editors actually keep, so focus points and depth of field can be refined later in **Final Cut Pro** and **Motion** on Mac. **Compressor** picks up conversion paths and new HEVC / MPEG-4 export presets to match.

## Creator Studio rides along

The same drop refreshes the **Apple Creator Studio** stack. **Pages**, **Numbers**, and **Keynote** get new templates and collaboration tooling; Numbers adds trigonometry, statistics, and logic functions; Pages on Mac gains a Screen View aimed at focused reading and writing. **Pixelmator Pro** on iPad finally gets **Select and Mask** that lived on Mac, plus new template collections. **Freeform** adds automatic Dark Mode for boards, folders for organization, and Writing Tools for handwritten notes.

Creator Studio remains **$12.99 per month** for the paid creative apps and premium features layered on the free productivity suite. Not every line item in today's notes requires the subscription — Freeform Dark Mode and folders are the usual "please stop making us wait for OS day-one" category — but the Final Cut / Pixelmator / template story is clearly aimed at people already in that orbit.

## Why it matters now

The iPhone 18 Pro's variable aperture was announced weeks ago. Shipping the free-standing pro capture app that actually exposes it today is late relative to launch, and obvious. If you bought the Pro for that Fusion Main camera and still open the stock Camera app for everything, this is the update that makes the hardware pitch real on a set, not just in a keynote reel.

Updates are rolling out today via the App Store and Mac software update paths. If you shoot on an 18 Pro and edit in Final Cut, install Final Cut Camera first, then check Final Cut Pro / Motion / Compressor for the matching Cinematic-mode edit hooks.
