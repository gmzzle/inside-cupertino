[AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/04/apple-plans-massive-64-inch-7-inch-screens-for-the-iphone-20?utm_source=rss) is reporting that Apple is prototyping 6.4-inch and 7-inch displays for the iPhone 20, citing a Weibo post from Digital Chat Station. The 6.4-inch part tracks—Apple has been slowly inching the Pro Max up from 6.1 to 6.7 inches over the years, and a jump to 6.4 for the standard Pro would follow that trajectory. Fine.

But a 7-inch iPhone? That's not a phone. That's the iPad mini's little brother. For context, the current iPad mini has an 8.3-inch display, and the original version launched at 7.9 inches. A 7-inch iPhone would overlap almost entirely with Apple's smallest tablet, which already struggles to justify its existence in the lineup. Why would Apple want to cannibalize the one product category where it still doesn't have serious Android competition?

The timing is also weird. The iPhone 20 is ostensibly the 2026 anniversary model—though calling it that feels like a stretch since Apple already did the nostalgia play with the iPhone X. If Apple wanted to make a statement with screen size, it already has the iPhone Fold in the pipeline, which will presumably unfold to something tablet-adjacent anyway. A 7-inch slab would just be a worse version of that without the party trick.

My guess is that if Apple is testing a 7-inch display, it's either for an internal experiment that'll never ship, or it's destined for something else entirely—maybe a future iPad mini refresh, or some hybrid device that hasn't leaked yet. The iPhone 20 Pro Max will probably get bigger, sure. But a 7-inch iPhone Pro Max Max isn't happening, and if it did, it would be the worst product decision Apple's made since the butterfly keyboard.
