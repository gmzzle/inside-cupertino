[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/24/meta-muse-instagram-dms/) reports that Meta's Muse AI is expanding into Instagram DMs, where it can organize your saved posts into collections, build travel itineraries, and compile grocery lists from recipes. It's rolling out in the U.S. and Canada first.

The move makes sense from a friction standpoint. Instagram's saved posts have always been a black hole—you tap the bookmark, then never look at it again. If Muse can actually parse what you've saved and group it intelligently, that's genuinely useful. The DM integration also means you don't need to context-switch between apps, which is how Meta thinks people will actually use this thing instead of forgetting it exists.

What's notable here is how much Meta is betting that people want AI woven directly into social apps, not siloed in separate chat interfaces. Apple's taking the opposite approach with Apple Intelligence—system-level, but cautious about injection into Messages or Photos in ways that feel invasive. Meta's going full send, putting Muse conversations right next to your actual friends in the DMs list. That's either convenient or creepy depending on how comfortable you are with Meta's AI watching everything you save and like.

The grocery list and travel itinerary features sound borrowed straight from Pinterest's playbook, except Meta has way more behavioral data to work with. If Muse can look at your saved restaurant posts and automatically build a weekend itinerary with realistic timing and transit, that's legitimately more useful than anything Siri's doing right now. But it also means giving Meta even more explicit permission to analyze your interests and habits in granular detail.

Meta's clearly trying to make Muse feel essential before Apple's AI tools mature enough to compete on convenience. Whether that works depends entirely on whether people trust Meta enough to let an AI agent rifle through their saved posts.
