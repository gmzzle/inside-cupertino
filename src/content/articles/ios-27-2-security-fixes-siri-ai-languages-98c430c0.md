While everyone was reading about this morning's [new iPad mini](/articles/new-ipad-mini-oled-a20-pro-ip67-7f579175/), Apple shipped the first big update to this fall's software. [iOS 27.2 and iPadOS 27.2](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/apple-releases-ios-27-2/) arrived Monday alongside macOS Golden Gate 27.2, watchOS 27.2, tvOS 27.2, visionOS 27.2 and new HomePod software. It's available for every iPhone that runs iOS 27, including the iPhone Duo.

The headline feature is Siri AI in more languages. The reason to install it today is security.

## Why you should update now

[MacRumors counts more than 160 security fixes](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/ios-27-2-security-fixes/) in iOS 27.2 and iPadOS 27.2, including an Apple Intelligence flaw that could let a remote attacker access sensitive data, a Hide My Email bug that could leak your real address, more than 30 WebKit fixes and roughly 15 kernel fixes. Across all of Monday's releases, [9to5Mac tallies at least 329 unique vulnerabilities](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-addressed-at-least-329-unique-security-vulnerabilities-across-todays-system-updates/).

Six of the fixes credit Calif, the team that jailbroke the iPhone Duo on launch day. Calif researcher Stefan Esser has said "most" of the flaws behind that jailbreak are fixed in 27.2, per MacRumors. Apple hasn't said any of these bugs are being actively exploited, but now that the details are public, an unpatched phone is an easier target.

If you're holding off on iOS 27, Apple also released iOS 26.7.2 with many of the same fixes, and iOS 18.7.11 for the iPhone XS, XS Max and XR, according to [Apple's security releases page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100). One caveat from that page: once you install an iOS update, you can't downgrade.

## What's new

- **Siri AI speaks more languages.** Siri AI, still labeled beta, now works in French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish in addition to English, per [Apple's announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/). MacRumors reports Chinese (Traditional), Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese are coming later this year. It's still not available in the EU or mainland China.
- **Siri remembers.** Siri AI can recall past conversations, so you can ask about something it told you last week. It can also pull from more personal context, like photos in Messages, Calendar attendees, voicemails, and Safari history.
- **Excluded Apps.** One setting now controls which apps Siri, Search and suggestions can see.
- **Suggestions in Messages.** iOS can offer to turn a conversation into an action, like adding a dinner to Calendar or a task to Reminders.
- **Apple TV app profiles.** Everyone in the house gets their own profile and recommendations.
- **Multiview on iPhone.** You can watch up to four live sports streams at once for events included with Apple TV, such as MLS and Formula 1 in the U.S.
- **Group FaceTime Dual Capture.** Streaming front and back cameras at once now works in group calls, and you can add a third-party sign language interpreter or live captioner to a call.
- **A hidden watermark on AI images.** Images made with Image Playground, and some photos edited with Apple Intelligence, now carry Google's SynthID watermark. In MacRumors' testing, Extend and Reframe edits were watermarked, while small Clean Up edits were not. There's no way to check for it on the iPhone itself.
- **Smaller touches.** Apple Music gets a new icon and branding, Podcasts splits its Home tab into New Episodes and Continue Playing, and Shared Albums in Photos can flag duplicates and warn before an album expires.

## For iPhone Duo owners

The Duo gets its own release notes, and two Duo-only additions: **Apple Pencil support** and **Edge Light**, which lights up your face on video calls.

What 27.2 doesn't claim to do is fix the inner-display cracks some early owners have reported along the hinge. Apple told MacRumors on Monday that a "small number" of units are affected and that owners should contact AppleCare for a replacement, without saying what causes it. If you're following that story, our [iFixit teardown recap](/articles/ifixit-iphone-duo-teardown-screen-cracks-hinge-decefc57/) covers the leading theories.

## The rest of the lineup

- **HomePod 27.2** adds Sound Glow, which makes the top of a HomePod or HomePod mini pulse with color from the album art, plus the option to use a HomePod as your Mac's speaker.
- **tvOS 27.2** supports four-HomePod surround sound with the upcoming Apple TV 4K, which arrives November 13. The HomePod mini 2 launches this Friday, October 30.
- **watchOS 27.2** brings the Siri AI memory features and what Apple calls faster Siri responses on the watch.
- **AirPods Pro hearing aid** support arrives in Alberta, British Columbia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Saskatchewan.

## How to get it

Go to **Settings > General > Software Update**. On a Mac, it's **System Settings > General > Software Update**. If you've been putting updates off, this is the one not to skip.
