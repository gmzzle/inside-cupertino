The M6 Macs got the headlines this morning, but Apple's [other Tuesday press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-creator-studio-adds-powerful-new-ai-features-and-even-more-creative-tools/) may matter more to the people who'll actually buy them. Apple Creator Studio, its subscription bundle of pro creative apps, is getting what Apple's Brent Chiu-Watson calls "our most exciting update since the launch of Apple Creator Studio." It arrives on the App Store in November as a free update for existing subscribers.

The short version: built-in effects for video editors, natural-language image editing, and AI assistants inside Logic Pro, Keynote, Pages and Numbers. Here's what's in it, app by app.

## Final Cut Pro and Motion

- **MotionVFX is built in.** Final Cut Pro for Mac now includes MotionVFX content: templates, graphics, titles, transitions, plus tools for rotoscoping, tracking and color grading. Apple says editors get "thousands of elements."
- **Stock footage inside the app.** Apple's curated Content Hub now carries video and is available directly in Final Cut Pro, alongside photos and backgrounds.
- **Pixelmator Pro round trips.** Open any image from the timeline in Pixelmator Pro, edit it, and the changes save back to the project with no reimporting.
- **Text-to-image in the project.** Editors can generate images from text descriptions without leaving Final Cut Pro.
- **Smaller wins editors will notice.** More keyframe easing options, the ability to disconnect clips from the primary storyline, and 23 new transcription languages for captions and search.
- **Motion and Compressor.** Motion gets MotionVFX plug-ins for color grading, 3D camera tracking and one-click selection, plus a new Displacement filter. Compressor can now encode Cinematic mode video shot on iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo.

## Pixelmator Pro: Generate Fill

This is Apple's answer to generative fill. Circle an area, or use the existing selection tools, and describe the change you want. Apple says it matches lighting, perspective and texture, and upscales the result with Super Resolution so it stays sharp. Generated content can be merged into a layer or added as a new layer with a mask you can keep refining.

## Logic Pro

- **An intelligent assistant (beta) on Mac.** Apple says it's powered by a combination of Apple-built and third-party models, and can help start a song, set up a vocal track from a single prompt, or refine a project in progress.
- **A redesigned Browser.** Loops, instruments and patches in one place, with on-device "music understanding" that tags your sounds so you can search your whole library or drag in a loop to find matching audio. Apple's summary says it includes a Splice library.
- **Stem Splitter does drums.** You can now isolate, replace or rebalance drum parts without the original multitracks.
- **A new look.** Logic Pro on Mac and iPad gets a Liquid Glass redesign.
- **Extras.** A Producer Pack from Sergio George with Latin jazz and salsa loops, and Voice Memo Studio Sound to cut room noise in recordings from supported iPhones.

## Keynote, Pages and Numbers

Subscribers get an intelligent assistant (beta) that Apple says is built on Apple models and OpenAI and understands the document you're in. In Keynote it helps design and animate slides, in Pages it drafts and restructures text, and in Numbers it calculates, suggests charts and builds tables.

The more interesting piece for power users is **MCP support**. MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is the open standard many AI tools use to connect to apps. Apple says it lets outside AI agents read content, make edits and run automated actions in Keynote, Pages and Numbers. Keynote also adds the Keynote Interchange Format (.keyx), an open JSON-based file format Apple says is meant to be readable by external tools and AI agents. Freeform gets text styles and background colors now, with Pixelmator Pro editing and text-to-shape generation arriving in December.

## The fine print

- **English and new software only.** Per Apple's footnotes, the Logic Pro assistant needs macOS 27 on an Apple Intelligence-capable Mac. The Keynote, Pages and Numbers assistant needs iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27 on an Apple Intelligence-capable device. Both are U.S. English only for now, and MCP support requires macOS 27.
- **These are subscriber features.** The free versions of Keynote, Pages, Numbers and Freeform still ship with every iPhone, Mac and iPad, but the new intelligence features are described as being for subscribers.
- **Pricing.** $12.99 a month or $129 a year in the U.S., with a one-month free trial. Buy a new Mac or qualifying iPad and you can get three months free. Education pricing is $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and up to six family members can share it. If you'd rather not subscribe, the one-time-purchase versions are still on the Mac App Store, including Final Cut Pro at $299.99 and Logic Pro at $199.99.

## Is it worth it?

If you already subscribe, this is a free and fairly large upgrade, especially for Final Cut Pro editors who get the MotionVFX library at no extra cost. If you were on the fence, the timing is pointed: Apple is pairing this with new M6 Macs that it pitches on on-device AI, and a new Mac purchase gets you three months free to try it. The catch is the AI assistants, which are betas, English-only and need the newest OS. Judge those after they ship in November, not from a press release.
