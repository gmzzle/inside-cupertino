[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/12/back-to-school-accessory/) rounded up Amazon's "back to school" accessory sale this week, and it's the usual August ritual: retailers slap a scholastic label on whatever inventory they need to move. Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones for $248? Sure, students use headphones. A $1,200 Samsung 32-inch Smart Monitor? That's a stretch. A 55-inch Frame TV for $898? Come on.

The discounts themselves are real enough. The Sonos Ace is down to $299 from $399, which is notable because Sonos almost never discounts new hardware this aggressively six months in. That suggests the Ace didn't sell as well as they hoped, which tracks - it's a $449 headphone in a market where Sony and Bose own the high end and everyone else races to the bottom. At $299 it starts to make sense, assuming you're already in the Sonos ecosystem and care about the home theater tie-in.

The Samsung monitor deal is legitimately good if you've been waiting for a display with built-in smart TV apps and USB-C, which is a specific use case but not a crazy one. The M9 started at $1,600 and hasn't moved much since launch. Now it's $400 off, which makes it almost competitive with LG's Studio Display knockoffs. Almost.

What's funny is how far "back to school" has drifted from actual school supplies. It used to mean notebooks and backpacks. Then it meant laptops. Now it's sleep earbuds and luxury TVs, because August is when retailers need to clear summer inventory before the fall iPhone launch eats all the consumer spend. Apple doesn't participate in this charade anymore - they killed their education sale's luster years ago - so the accessory makers picked up the slack.

If you were already planning to buy any of this stuff, the timing's fine. Just don't pretend your Frame TV is a school expense.
