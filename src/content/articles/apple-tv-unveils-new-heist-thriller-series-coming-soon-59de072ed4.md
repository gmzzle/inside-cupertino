[9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/13/apple-tv-unveils-new-heist-thriller-series-coming-soon/) reported today that Apple TV+ is launching *12 12 12*, a heist thriller premiering in November. The trailer dropped this morning, and it's got that slick Ocean's Eleven energy—split-second timing, elaborate cons, a crew with specialties.

Apple's been chasing this format for a while now. They want their *Lupin*, their *Money Heist*. The streaming wars have proven that heist shows travel well internationally and generate the kind of week-to-week buzz that Apple desperately needs. *Presumed Innocent* did solid numbers this summer, but it was a limited series. Apple needs shows that can run multiple seasons and keep subscribers around.

The title *12 12 12* presumably references some kind of deadline or vault combination or triple-cross structure. It's the kind of gimmick that works if the writing is sharp and falls apart if it's not. Apple's track record on thrillers is mixed—*Hijack* was taut and well-executed, but *The Crowded Room* was a mess. The difference usually comes down to whether they let the creators cook or whether too many executives got in the kitchen.

What's interesting is the timing. November puts this right in the holiday window when people are scrolling for something new. Apple clearly thinks it's got something here, or they'd bury it in January. Whether it's actually good or just expensive-looking remains the question. Apple can make anything look prestige. Making it land is harder.

If *12 12 12* works, expect Apple to order three more heist shows by spring.
