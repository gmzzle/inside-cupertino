Apple has shown its Black Friday hand, and there are no price cuts in it. Just Apple Gift Cards. That can still be a good deal. But for a lot of shoppers this year, it won't be the best one.

Here's what Apple is offering, what it leaves out, and a simple way to decide where to buy.

## What Apple is offering

Apple's four-day shopping event runs **Friday, November 27 through Monday, November 30**. [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/20/apple-reveals-2026-black-friday-deals/) reports it's running in many countries, including the U.S., Canada, the U.K., and Australia.

In the U.S., you get an Apple Gift Card when you buy an eligible product. Apple's [event page](https://www.apple.com/shop/gifts/shopping-event) lists the top amount for each category:

- **Mac:** up to $300
- **iPad:** up to $125
- **iPhone:** up to $100
- **Apple Watch:** up to $100
- **AirPods:** up to $75
- **Beats:** up to $75
- **HomePod:** up to $50
- **Accessories:** up to $25

Note the words "up to." Apple's fine print says card values vary by product, so not every Mac gets $300.

There's also a streaming deal that doesn't require buying anything. New and qualified returning U.S. subscribers can get the Apple TV streaming service for **$6.99 a month for up to six months**, down from the usual $14.99, according to MacRumors. That one is live now and runs until 8 p.m. Pacific on November 30.

## What it leaves out

This is the big catch. Per MacRumors, many of Apple's newest products aren't part of the gift card offer, including:

- iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max
- iPhone Duo
- MacBook Neo
- The latest iPad mini
- Apple TV 4K
- iMac and Mac Studio

Refurbished products don't count, and you can't stack the offer with education pricing. Apple's terms also exclude purchases tied to a carrier and Apple Upgrade purchases.

So if the thing on your list is this fall's headline product, Apple's own sale probably won't help you.

## A gift card isn't cash

A $100 gift card isn't the same as $100 off. You pay full price today, and the card can only be spent on a future Apple purchase.

That's fine if you were going to buy a case, AirPods, or another Apple product soon anyway. If not, it's money you've pre-committed to Apple, which is exactly the point from Apple's side.

Meanwhile, the instant discounts at other retailers are already here. MacRumors' deal tracking shows Amazon prices like these right now:

- **M5 MacBook Air:** $250 off most models
- **14-inch M6 MacBook Pro:** $150 off
- **MacBook Neo:** $50 off, down to $649 (excluded from Apple's offer entirely)
- **M4 iPad Air:** $100 off most models
- **AirPods Pro 3:** $179.99, about $69 off
- **Apple Watch Series 12:** $70 off, new all-time lows

Line those up against Apple's cards and the math mostly favors the instant discount. Apple's AirPods card tops out at $75, which is only a few dollars more than Amazon's cash discount, and you can't spend it on anything else. A Mac buyer who happens to land a $300 card could come out ahead of the MacBook Air discount, but only if that card turns into something they actually want.

## Where iPhone buyers should look instead

Apple's gift card deal skips the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo, and the carriers are going the other way. MacRumors reports T-Mobile and AT&T are advertising up to $1,200 off those phones with an eligible trade-in.

Read that fine print, though. Those savings come as bill credits spread over 36 months, and they're tied to specific plans and trade-in values. Before you sign, check what happens to the remaining credits if you switch carriers early. It's a three-year commitment dressed up as a holiday sale.

## How to decide

- **Buy from Apple if** the product you want is eligible, you know what you'll spend the card on, and you'd rather buy directly from Apple, in a store or online, including custom configurations that other retailers may not carry.
- **Buy from Amazon or Best Buy if** you want the lowest price you pay today, or the product is excluded from Apple's offer.
- **Go through a carrier only if** you're already happy with that carrier and plan to stay for three years.
- **Grab the Apple TV deal if** there's a show you've been meaning to catch up on. Six months at under half price is the cleanest Apple deal of the bunch, and you can cancel before the price goes back up.

Apple rarely cuts prices in its own stores, and this year's event is a reminder of that. The company would rather give you store credit than lower the price. Sometimes that works in your favor. This year, for most people, the better deal is somewhere else.
