[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/22/best-apple-deals-of-the-week-8-21-26/) rounded up this week's Apple deals, and for once there's something worth paying attention to. Best Buy's 60th anniversary sale has the AirPods Max 2 at $429, which is $120 off and the lowest price we've seen since they launched last year. If you've been waiting for Apple's most divisive headphones to hit a number that doesn't feel insulting, this is it.

The M4 iPad Air is also $100 off, landing the base 11-inch model at $499. That's the best summer pricing on what's quietly become the most sensible iPad in the lineup - M4 power without the mini-LED tax of the Pro, and enough screen for actual work. Apple doesn't discount its own iPads this aggressively outside of back-to-school season, so Best Buy doing it in late August is notable. No membership required for most of these deals, which is a nice change from the usual retailer games.

The AirPods Max 2 deserve the scrutiny here. Apple finally added USB-C and better active noise cancellation, but kept the same divisive design and that baffling case. At $549, they're a tough sell against Sony and Bose. At $429, they're actually competitive, especially if you're already using an iPhone and want the seamless switching that only Apple's H2 chip delivers. The sound quality was never the problem - it was the price and the lack of a real carrying solution.

Best Buy also has the usual smattering of older AirPods models and previous-gen iPads on sale, but those feel less urgent. The AirPods Pro 2 have been hovering around $189 for months, and last year's iPad discounts aren't worth chasing when the current models are this cheap. If you're in the market for either the Max or the M4 Air, this week's actually the time to move.
