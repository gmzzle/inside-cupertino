Apple shipped iOS 27.2 this week, and tucked inside is a feature that should've launched with the iPhone Duo back in 2025: Multiview for the Apple TV app. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/29/ios-27-2-adds-new-video-feature-thats-perfect-for-iphone-duo/) reported the addition, calling it "especially perfect" for the Duo's panoramic display.

They're not wrong. The Duo's 8-inch unfolded screen has been begging for split-view video since day one. Picture-in-picture felt like a band-aid. Multiview—two or more live streams side by side, synced audio switching between them—is exactly what that hardware was built for. NFL Sunday Ticket subscribers can now watch two games at once without squinting at an iPad. MLS Season Pass fans can follow multiple matches. It's the feature Apple should've demoed on stage when they announced the thing.

But here's the problem: it's October 2026. The Duo has been on the market for sixteen months. Apple had Multiview working on Apple TV 4K and iPad Pro since early 2024. Porting it to a foldable iPhone running the same tvOS video stack shouldn't have taken this long. The delay makes it look like Apple didn't prioritize the Duo's signature use case until sales data forced their hand.

The feature itself works well enough. You can watch up to four streams simultaneously, though realistically two is the sweet spot on a phone-sized display. Audio follows whichever feed you tap. It's limited to Apple's own sports packages for now—no third-party apps, no YouTube streams, no creative hacks. Classic Apple: elegant within the walled garden, useless outside it.

Still, better late than never. If you bought a Duo hoping it would eventually justify its $1,799 price tag, this moves the needle. If you passed because the software wasn't there yet, well, you were right to wait.
