[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/04/airpods-pro-3-179-prime-day/) spotted AirPods Pro 3 dropping to $179 on Amazon for early Prime Day, down from the $249 launch price. That's $70 off a product that shipped just over a year ago, which is legitimately good if you've been waiting.

The question is whether the Pro 3's headline features—2x better ANC, heart rate sensing, live translation—actually justify buying them over the previous generation, which routinely shows up around $169 refurbished. The ANC improvement is real and noticeable if you commute or fly often. The revised fit helps with long wear. But heart rate sensing? You probably already have an Apple Watch doing that job better. Live translation is clever in theory but borderline gimmicky in practice unless you're regularly having extended conversations in languages you don't speak, and even then your phone does the same thing with better accuracy.

Apple positioned the Pro 3 as health-forward hardware, leaning into workout tracking and biometric data. That makes sense as a product strategy—AirPods are already on your head, might as well grab more health stats—but the execution feels like a feature looking for a problem. Most people buying AirPods just want excellent sound, solid ANC, and seamless Apple ecosystem integration. The Pro 3 delivers all that. The extras are nice to have, not reasons to upgrade.

At $179, though, this is the right price if you're buying new. It's the lowest the Pro 3 has hit since June, and likely the best you'll see until Black Friday. If your current AirPods are dying or you're coming from nothing, grab them. If you're on Pro 2s and wondering if it's worth the jump, it's not unless ANC is a daily pain point. The marginal gains don't justify even a discounted upgrade when what you have still works fine.
