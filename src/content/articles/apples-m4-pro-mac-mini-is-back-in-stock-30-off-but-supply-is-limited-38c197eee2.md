[AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/31/apples-m4-pro-mac-mini-is-back-in-stock-30-off-but-supply-is-limited?utm_source=rss) reported that Amazon has the M4 Pro Mac mini back in stock at $1,570—$30 off and shipping in 1-2 days. Meanwhile, Apple's own store has been showing 3-4 week delays on the same configuration for most of the past month.

This isn't normal. The Mac mini has historically been Apple's steadiest, most boring product line from a supply perspective. It doesn't have the launch frenzy of an iPhone or the boutique scarcity of a Mac Pro. It just... exists, available, ready to ship. Not anymore. The M4 Pro models—especially the 24GB/512GB config that hits the sweet spot for developers and AI tinkering—have been consistently sold out or backordered since late spring.

The obvious culprit is AI agent demand. Developers building and testing local AI workflows want headless machines with enough unified memory to run models without choking. The M4 Pro's 24GB is the entry point where that becomes feasible, and at $1,600ish, it's cheaper than speccing out a comparable Mac Studio. Apple didn't design this machine for that use case, but the market found it anyway.

What's strange is that Amazon keeps getting inventory Apple itself doesn't seem to have. Either Apple's prioritizing third-party channels to avoid the optics of empty shelves on its own site, or there's a deliberate allocation strategy to keep resellers stocked while direct orders slip. Neither explanation is great. The former suggests Apple's embarrassed about demand it can't meet. The latter suggests it's playing games with its own customers.

If you've been waiting for an M4 Pro mini, grab this one. Not because the discount is life-changing, but because the alternative is watching Apple's site tick from "3 weeks" to "4 weeks" while you wonder if they're even still making them.
