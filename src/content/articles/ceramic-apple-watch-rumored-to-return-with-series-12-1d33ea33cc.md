[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/20/ceramic-apple-watch-to-return-with-series-12/) is reporting that Apple plans to resurrect the ceramic Apple Watch with the Series 12 this fall, citing leaker Kosutami. If true, it would mark the return of a finish Apple has now killed off twice—once after the Series 3 in 2018, and again after the Series 5 in 2020.

The ceramic Watch was always a weird product. It launched at $1,249 in 2016 as a less embarrassing replacement for the $10,000 gold Edition nobody bought. Apple pitched it as premium—four times harder than stainless steel, made from compressed zirconia powder, polished with diamond slurry. It looked great in white, genuinely distinctive in a way the stainless models never were. But it was also heavy, prone to shattering if you actually dropped it (hardness isn't toughness), and cost nearly as much as the original gold model had been ridiculous for.

Apple clearly never figured out who this was for. It added a gray ceramic option in 2017, then killed the whole Edition tier in 2018. Brought it back in 2019 alongside titanium—which immediately made more sense as the premium option—then killed ceramic again in 2020. Titanium stayed. Ceramic collectors started paying four figures on eBay. That should tell you something about the market size.

So why bring it back now? Maybe Apple thinks six years is long enough for people to forget it flopped twice. Maybe they've got manufacturing capacity to fill and want another SKU above the $799 titanium model. Maybe they genuinely think there's pent-up demand from the collector crowd, though that seems like a rounding error on Apple's revenue. Or maybe it's just cheaper to produce now and they want to test whether a distinctive material can command a margin in 2026 the way it couldn't in 2020.

Either way, if this rumor holds, expect it to sell poorly, look beautiful, and get quietly discontinued again around the Series 14. Some ideas are better as collectibles than products.
