It's already Friday, October 23 in Sydney and Auckland, which means Apple's first foldable is no longer a review unit. [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/) and [The Apple Post](https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/10/22/72351/first-iphone-duo-deliveries-arrive-to-customers-in-australia-and-new-zealand/) report that the first pre-ordered iPhone Duos are reaching customers in Australia and New Zealand, with first impressions and photos already showing up on forums and social media. Deliveries roll west from there through Asia, the Middle East and Europe before reaching North America later in the day.

The hardware story has been told in the reviews. The question buyers are about to run into is a software one: how many of their apps actually know what to do with a 7.6-inch folding screen.

## The launch, by the numbers

- **Price:** from $1,999 in the US, in star white and night sky, with 256GB to 2TB of storage, per The Apple Post.
- **Screens:** a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display, running iOS 27.1 on Apple's A20 Pro chip.
- **Getting one:** online shipping estimates in the US slipped to January 25 shortly after pre-orders opened on October 16 and have stayed there, according to MacRumors. Apple says stores in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and a handful of other countries will have a limited number of walk-in units on launch day, and none in markets including France, China, Japan and South Korea. Apple's site tells shoppers to check back Saturday, October 24 for pickup reservations.

In Sydney, [Tech Guide](https://www.techguide.com.au/news/mobiles-news/worlds-first-iphone-duo-sold-in-australia-and-its-being-given-away-to-help-the-homeless/) counted a line of about 150 people outside the George Street Apple Store, led by a buyer who had waited since Monday and says he's giving his Duo away to raise money for a homelessness charity.

## Apple is putting a badge on the apps that are ready

On Thursday, Apple published a [Newsroom post](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/developers-bring-incredible-new-app-experiences-to-iphone-duo/) showing off apps built for the Duo and announced that the App Store will mark them with a **"Made for iPhone Duo"** badge on their product pages, alongside new editorial collections going live Friday.

The badge exists because the gap between a ready app and an unready one is easy to see. [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/22/made-for-iphone-duo-app-store-label/) breaks down how apps behave depending on which version of Apple's developer tools they were built with:

- **Built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later:** the app can fill the entire inner display, with its toolbar running vertically below the status bar.
- **Built with the iOS 27 SDK:** the app fills most of the inner display but stays clear of the status bar on the right edge.
- **Built with the iOS 26 SDK or earlier:** the app runs, but sits in the middle of the inner screen with lots of empty space on each side.

MacRumors videographer Dan Barbera, who got a Duo a day early, described those unoptimized apps as "usable, but it's not a good experience," with thick black bars on the sides.

## Who's ready on day one

Apple's list includes Netflix, Prime Video, Paramount+, the MLB app, Zoom, Slack, Salesforce, Lyft, Fantastical, Things, Bear, Flighty, Instagram, NYT Cooking, Yelp, DoorDash, OpenTable and United Airlines, among others. Some of the clever bits lean on the fold itself: Netflix plays video on the top half when the Duo is propped open like a tiny laptop, and The Verge notes Prime Video can show cast details on the bottom half while a movie plays on top.

Apple's own apps are catching up too. Keynote, Pages, Numbers, GarageBand and TestFlight all picked up Duo support this week, per MacRumors. The Duo also brings Split View to the iPhone for the first time, so two apps can run side by side, and Apple says Microsoft Word and Outlook will get desktop-style layouts "in the coming weeks."

Not everything on the list is polished. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1016526/apple-made-for-iphone-duo-badge-app-store) points out that Nilay Patel still ran into bugs in some apps labeled as optimized, Instagram among them.

## What this means

- **Check your must-have apps before you line up.** If the apps you live in aren't on Apple's list, expect black bars on the big screen for now. The badge on each App Store page is the quickest tell.
- **This should get better fast.** Barbera expects a wave of app updates starting Friday, and Apple says more will roll out through the year. [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3267241/apple-to-iphone-duo-buyers-we-promise-your-favorite-apps-will-look-great-eventually.html) notes the same catch-up happened with the iPad, iPhone 5 and iPhone X.
- **Early buyers are paying to be early.** At $1,999 with compromises on the camera and no Face ID, the Duo's value leans heavily on what the inner screen can do. Right now that's a promise for some apps and a reality for others.
