[AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/10/20/owc-has-1tb-m5-ipad-pros-on-sale-for-1499-300-off?utm_source=rss) reported that OWC is selling pre-owned 11-inch M5 iPad Pros with 1TB of storage for $1,499, which is $300 under the new price. That's the entire thing. It's a deal on a refurb.

But let's talk about who this is actually for, because the 1TB iPad Pro occupies a weird spot in Apple's lineup. The base M5 iPad Pro starts at $999 with 256GB, which is plenty for most people who use iPads like iPads—browsing, notes, streaming, occasional Procreate work. The 1TB model exists for the small population doing serious video editing in LumaFusion or maintaining massive Lightroom libraries on the go. Those people know who they are, and they're probably already mentally justifying this purchase.

The M5 chip is overkill for almost everything iPadOS currently allows, which has been true since the M1 iPad Pro and remains true today. Apple keeps putting Mac-class processors in iPads while keeping the software on a leash, so you get benchmark scores that smoke most laptops but still can't run Final Cut or Xcode. The storage matters more than the chip here—if you need a terabyte, you need a terabyte, and the M5 just happens to come along for the ride.

OWC's refurbs are generally solid. They're not Apple Certified Refurbished, but OWC has been around forever and their return policy is reasonable. At $1,499 you're paying less than a new 512GB model ($1,299) while getting double the storage, assuming you're comfortable with pre-owned. That math works if storage is your constraint.

The real question is whether you should wait for an M6 model or just pull the trigger now. The M5 iPad Pro is going to be fast enough for years, and iPadOS updates tend to age out devices based on RAM and feature compatibility, not raw compute. If you need the storage today, this is probably the best price you'll see on a 1TB M5 for a while.
