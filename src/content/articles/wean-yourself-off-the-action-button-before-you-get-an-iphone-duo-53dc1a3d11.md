The iPhone Duo ditched the Action Button. [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/inside/ios/tips/wean-yourself-off-the-action-button-before-you-get-an-iphone-duo?utm_source=rss) is already coaching people on the workaround: swap out a lock screen control with a Shortcut that does the same thing. Smart move for people who actually mapped that button to something useful. The question is how many of you that actually is.

The Action Button launched on the iPhone 15 Pro in September 2023 with all the fanfare of a feature Apple clearly thought would matter. A physical button you could customize! Silent mode, camera, flashlight, Voice Memo, whatever. In practice, it was mostly a weirder version of the old ring/silent switch. The interaction model was clunky from day one—press it, get a pop-up telling you to press *longer*, then maybe it does the thing. Apple never refined that. They just shipped it again on the 16 Pro and moved on.

Now the Duo launches without it, and the fix is basically "use a lock screen shortcut instead." Which is fine! Lock screen controls in iOS 18 are surprisingly good. You lose the physical tactility, sure, but you gain flexibility—swap functions on the fly, no Accessibility Settings spelunking required. For most people who set the Action Button to Camera or Flashlight and forgot about it, this is a lateral move at worst.

The real tell here is that Apple didn't fight to keep it. The Duo is *thin*—thinner than anything they've shipped since the iPhone 6. Every millimeter counts. If the Action Button were genuinely essential, they'd have found a way. They didn't, which suggests even Apple's own usage data said it was skippable. That's not a knock on people who loved it. It's just the math of what gets cut when priorities collide.

If you're holding an iPhone 15 Pro or 16 Pro right now, try this: don't touch the Action Button for a week. If you don't miss it, the Duo will be fine.
